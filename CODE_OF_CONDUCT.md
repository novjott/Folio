# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Folio is intended to be a welcoming, respectful and constructive open-source project.

We want students, developers and contributors from different backgrounds and experience levels to feel comfortable participating, learning and sharing ideas.

We are committed to maintaining a community where people can collaborate without harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.

## Expected Behaviour

All community members are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and consideration.
- Be welcoming to newcomers and people with different levels of experience.
- Provide constructive feedback rather than personal criticism.
- Assume good intentions while remaining open to correction.
- Respect differing opinions and technical approaches.
- Accept reasonable criticism and feedback.
- Focus discussions on improving Folio and helping its users.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, bullying, threats or intimidation.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Personal attacks, insults or deliberate humiliation.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or collaboration.
- Repeatedly ignoring reasonable requests to keep discussions respectful.
- Any other conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

Disagreement about technical decisions is acceptable. Disrespectful treatment of people is not.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and pull requests.
- Discussions and code reviews.
- Project-related communication channels.
- Other spaces where someone is officially representing Folio.

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, contact the repository maintainers privately through an appropriate contact method listed on GitHub.

Please include relevant context or links where possible. Avoid publishing private information in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible. Information will only be shared when necessary to address the situation.

## Enforcement

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may:

1. Ask someone to stop or correct inappropriate behaviour.
2. Remove inappropriate content from project spaces.
3. Temporarily restrict participation.
4. Suspend or permanently remove participation in the project.

Responses will consider the seriousness and context of the behaviour. Repeated or severe violations may result in stronger action.

## Fairness and Responsibility

Maintainers are expected to apply this Code of Conduct consistently and to act respectfully themselves.

If a maintainer is involved in a report, that person should not be solely responsible for deciding the outcome.

## Acknowledgement

By participating in the Folio project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a constructive community.

Our goal is to build useful software for students while making collaboration a positive experience for everyone.
